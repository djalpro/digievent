About us

We are a team of planners, designers and tech lovers who turn ideas into memorable events.

Our story

DigiEvent was born from a simple observation: organising an event is exciting, but managing it is exhausting. Spreadsheets, emails, suppliers, tickets… everything is scattered.

We decided to bring it all together. Today we combine the human touch of a traditional event agency with digital tools that make planning simple, transparent and measurable.
